# Privacy

*Last updated: 2026-10-19*

Deskair does not collect, send or share any data. It has no account, no analytics and no network connection of its own.

- **What it reads:** the CO₂, temperature, humidity, pressure and battery values of your Aranet4 sensor, over Bluetooth LE.
- **Where it keeps them:** only on your PC. Readings are saved as CSV files in `Documents\Deskair` (or the folder you choose in Settings), together with your settings. Logs for troubleshooting stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Deskair\logs` and are not sent anywhere.
- **Bluetooth:** used only to find and read your sensor. Deskair does not track your location.
- **Deleting your data:** delete the `Deskair` folder in Documents. Uninstalling the app does not delete it.

Deskair is an unofficial, independent open-source project. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the maker of Aranet4.
