History

History has to answer two questions: was this period good or bad, and when does the air go wrong? Week and Month become a grid with one row per day and one square per hour, so stuffy nights and bad afternoons show as patterns. Year uses the same grid with one square per day. Day compares the last 24 hours with a typical day. The ‹ › arrows step back through time. The concept draws CO₂; the other metrics would use the same layout with their own scale. Hover any square or the line for values.
