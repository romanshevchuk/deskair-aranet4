Deskair — zone states & History

Extension of Air_Pulse_Refined: hero card in all three CO₂ zones, and a reworked History tab.

1 · Hero card by CO₂ zone

Zone drives card tint, border, pill, advice panel and the app’s ring icon. The number stays near-black for legibility, and the zone is always also stated in words and an icon.

Dark theme: same layout, tints re-balanced (deep, low-chroma fills with lighter text). Switch the whole app in Settings → Theme.

3 · System notifications & tray icon

Windows toasts are drawn by Windows (card, fonts and corners are the system's); Deskair adds the zone-coloured ring next to the text and two buttons. Quiet by default: one toast per episode, a Snooze action, and a “fresh again” confirmation. The tray icon always updates, even with toasts off.

Tray icon states

Tray flyout and menu

Left-click opens the flyout, a quick glance without opening the window. Right-click opens the context menu. This matches how Windows 11 treats Wi-Fi, volume, battery and most modern tray apps. Hovering shows a tooltip. A double-click can open the full window (optional). The flyout has a filled “Open Deskair” button (clicking the value also opens the app), closes on click-away or Esc, and closing the main window keeps the tray icon running.

4 · Suggested storage for History

  • Keep raw samples briefly; the History tab only reads aggregates, so it opens instantly.
  • Store min / avg / max + sample count per bucket. Week and month averages are then exact (weighted by n), not an average of averages.
  • Daily rows also keep minutes spent in each CO₂ zone, which powers “time in zone”.
  • Airing events get their own small events.csv, so “time to clear” never needs raw data. Each room (device) has its own folder.
hourly.csv   (kept 90 days, ~2,160 rows)
ts,co2_min,co2_avg,co2_max,temp_min,temp_avg,temp_max,hum_min,hum_avg,hum_max,pres_min,pres_avg,pres_max,n
2026-10-08T19:00,1210,1388,1661,22.1,22.3,22.5,43,44,45,975.1,975.3,975.4,60

daily.csv    (kept forever, ~365 rows/yr)
date,[same min/avg/max columns],co2_good_min,co2_elev_min,co2_high_min,n

events.csv   (kept forever, a few rows a day)
ts_start,ts_end,co2_start,co2_end,minutes_to_good,room
2026-10-08T19:12,2026-10-08T19:23,1420,610,11,Office

%APPDATA%\Deskair\<room>\{hourly,daily,events}.csv   one folder per device

Airing feedback — recommendation

After a “ventilate” prompt, tell people whether it worked and how long it took. This builds the habit better than any chart. It appears as a toast, as ⌂ markers on the Live 24 h strip and History bars, and as a per-room “typical time to clear”. Call it “air improved”, not “window opened”, because the sensor can’t tell a window from a door or air conditioning.

5 · Feature notes

Short reference so nothing gets lost. Status: In design = shown in this file, Documented = described only, Planned = idea.

Implementation plan — highest-value features

Airing feedbackDocumented + mock
  • Detect: CO₂ ≥ 1,000 ppm and drops ≥ 150 ppm within 10 min. Event starts at the turning point; ends when below 1,000, when the slope flattens (< 10 ppm per 5 min), or after 45 min.
  • Store: one events.csv row per event; minutes_to_good is empty if never reached.
  • Show: “Fresh again” toast, ⌂ markers on the Live strip and History, room insight “typical time to clear” (median of the last 20 events).
Sleep view / night summaryIn design
  • Window: 22:00–07:00 (fixed in v1, configurable later). Derived from hourly.csv, no new file.
  • Metrics: average, peak and time, hours ≥ 1,000 ppm, average 02:00–05:00 (deep sleep).
  • Verdict: Good = avg < 1,000 and ≤ 1 h above; Poor = avg > 1,400 or ≥ 5 h above; otherwise Fair.
  • Where: History → Hours filter “☾ Night” (Day range = last night with the sleep card; Week, Month and Year = one average per night, so you can compare nights across a month or a year). Plus a morning toast at the first reading after 07:00.
NotificationsIn design
  • Trigger: zone entry after a 5 min dwell, so short spikes are ignored.
  • Throttle: one toast per episode. Re-notify only on escalation to High, or if still High after 60 min.
  • Respect: quiet hours and Snooze 1 h. “Fresh again” only if a toast was shown for that episode.
  • Tech: Windows toast with action buttons (Snooze, Open). Tray icon is independent of toast settings.
Click a bar to drill inDocumented only
  • Month bar → Month range at that month. Week bar → Week range ending that week. Day bar → Day range for that day.
  • Needs a date cursor in the view state and a breadcrumb “2026 › Sep › Sep 14” with Back.
  • Day view reads hourly.csv (90 days). Older days show daily data only, with an “hourly data expired” note.
Work-hours focusSetting in design
  • Setting: on/off, hours, days (Settings → Work hours). The same Hours filter in History exposes it as “Work”.
  • Effect: History shows a “Work hours” chip; averages and time-in-zone count only those hours.
  • Data: hourly rows cover 90 days. For older periods, daily.csv also stores *_avg_work and n_work, written at the daily rollover.

Other features

Recent-events stripIn design
  • Lives inside the Live chart card (no extra block). 24 h ribbon, one colour per 10 min by zone, ⌂ markers where the room was aired.
  • Data: interpolated from hourly.csv plus live samples. The ⌂ markers come from events.csv and appear once airing detection ships; the ribbon works without them. The hero stat “In the … zone” replaced “Since last airing” for the same reason.
  • Live chart ranges 1h / 6h / 24h use live samples for the last hour and hourly.csv before that. The ribbon is hidden on 24h because it would repeat the chart. Hovering shows a crosshair and the exact value and zone.
Multiple devicesIn design
  • Room chip in the title bar opens a menu with each room’s value and zone. Selecting a room switches Live, History and Settings context.
  • One data folder per device. Option for the tray icon to show the worst room.
  • Post-v1: an “All rooms” overview, only worth building once people commonly have 3+ sensors. The room chip and the flyout room switcher cover v1.
History gaps and syncIn design
  • Sync: on launch and on every reconnect, read the sensor’s stored history and merge it by timestamp. Sensor values fill missing rows; existing rows are never deleted. Live always has the last 24 h after a sync.
  • Gaps: a missing row is simply missing (no placeholder). Charts show a hatched “No data” band, with the length when wide enough. Hovering says “device not synced”.
  • Partial periods: a bar based on under 80% of its expected readings is drawn dashed and lighter, and the hover says “only N of M h recorded”.
  • Honest averages: averages use only existing readings. A chip in the stats card shows the coverage (“Based on 71% of expected readings · 10 days with no data”). It is hidden when data is complete.
  • Check: how many days the sensor keeps at the chosen interval, to word the “sync every few days” advice.
Alerts and Windows Do Not DisturbDocumented
  • Follow Windows Focus Assist / Do Not Disturb. Never override it.
  • When Windows is suppressing toasts, show a small “Alerts paused by Windows” indicator (tray tooltip and Settings → Alerts). The tray icon colour still updates.
Tray behaviourIn design
  • Left-click: flyout. Right-click: menu. Double-click: open window (optional). Hover: tooltip.
  • Closing the window keeps the tray running (Settings → Start-up).
  • Windows 11 hides new tray icons in the overflow (^). First run should show a tip: “Drag Deskair out of the overflow so it stays visible.”
Edge statesIn design
  • Stale: no reading for 3+ intervals (about 5 min) → banner, dimmed hero, amber footer.
  • Not connected: no reading for 10 min or Bluetooth off → empty state with recovery steps, grey tray icon. History stays available.
  • Low battery: ≤ 10% → banner.
  • First run: no paired sensor → onboarding; History shows “collecting data” with a 7-day progress bar.
Shareable report · Explanations · Colour-blind patternsPlanned
  • Report: week or month as image/PDF with time-in-zone.
  • Explanations: “What does 1,400 ppm mean?” popover.
  • Patterns or icons per zone in History charts.
