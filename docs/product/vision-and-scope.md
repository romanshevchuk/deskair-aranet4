# Vision and scope

*Last reviewed: 2026-10*

## Why this app exists

The Aranet4 phone app works, but opening it is a chore when you sit at a PC all day. A desktop app can show the air quality at a glance (tray icon), tell you when to open a window, and keep a richer, dashboard-style history.

## Target users

People who own an Aranet4: remote workers, parents (bedroom air), people with allergies or asthma, teachers and small-office managers. They want one answer ("should I open a window?") and proof that their changes worked.

## Version 1

| In v1 | Later (after v1) |
|---|---|
| Live: CO₂, temperature, humidity, pressure; 1h / 6h / 24h; hover readout | Airing feedback ("air improved in N min") |
| Tray icon, left-click flyout, right-click menu, toast alerts | Sleep verdicts and morning toast |
| History: Day / Week / Month / Year, Hours filter (All day, Night), group by, gap states | Click a bar to drill in |
| Settings, first run, stale / low battery / not connected states | Shareable report |
| Sensor sync, local storage, import from the phone app (.xlsx), export CSV | All-rooms view |
| English and Ukrainian | Editable CO₂ thresholds; work-hours filter |
| Per-device data folders (the interface for several rooms can follow) | |

## Platforms

Windows 10 (19041 and later) and Windows 11. Mica backdrop on Windows 11 only.

## Non-goals for v1

Cloud sync, accounts, telemetry, mobile app, other sensors, editable thresholds, work-hours logic.
