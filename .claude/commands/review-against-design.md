description: Compare an implemented area with the design docs
argument-hint: <area, for example Live, History, Settings, Tray>
---

Review the implementation of $ARGUMENTS against `docs/design/screens-and-states.md`, `docs/design/components.md`, `docs/design/design-tokens.md` and `docs/product/behaviour-rules.md`.

List, in this order: missing states, wrong colours or sizes (token names), missing or untranslated strings, accessibility gaps (`docs/design/accessibility.md`), and behaviour that differs from the rules. Do not change code; report only.
